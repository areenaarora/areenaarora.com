## Numerical and editorial checks

- The cutoffs do not imply that all members, or the averages, of the two deciles have the same ordering.
- Independently weighted bin averages reproduce higher bottom-decile means in the four comparator surveys than the Indian upper-decile consumption mean. This is a separate result with mixed welfare measures, not proof of the headline’s earnings wording.
- Do not label India’s upper consumption decile as the wealthiest households; wealth is a different stock measure.
- A cross-sectional distribution comparison cannot establish the effect of a proposed business-policy package. Attribute expert interpretation and evaluate it with separate evidence.
- Every invented number is labeled illustrative. Examples will be checked arithmetically.
