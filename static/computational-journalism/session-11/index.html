A threshold is not a group average

India’s P90 = $9.98/day

90% of Indians are at or below this value. The richest 10% are above it.

US P10 = $25.13/day

10% of Americans are at or below this value. The remaining 90% are above it.

Population ranks, not income bars. Selected World Bank PIP rows: India and US, 2022; 2021 PPP $ per person per day. These cutoffs vary by year.

What the data shows

The cutoff for entering India’s richest 10% is lower than the upper boundary of America’s poorest 10%.

What it does not show

That every person in India’s richest 10% earns less than every person in America’s poorest 10%.

A percentile tells you where a group begins or ends, not what everyone in that group earns.

Same dollars. Different measurements.

India

Consumption per person, per day

United States

Income per person, per day

Consumption measures what households use or spend. Income measures resources received. They are related, but not identical.

₹80,000/month ≠ $9.98/day

₹80,000: an individual earnings claim in nominal rupees.

$9.98: a percentile cutoff for per-person household consumption in 2021 PPP dollars.

A direct comparison requires matching definitions, populations, time periods and units.

Before comparing two numbers, check what each one actually measures.
