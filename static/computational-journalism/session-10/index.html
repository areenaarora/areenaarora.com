Six edits, one better story

  1. Replace the vague headline with a specific finding.
  2. Remove decoration with no purpose.
  3. Give type consistent roles.
  4. Make the strongest chart prominent.
  5. Improve reading width and spacing.
  6. Reflow the page for mobile.

Keep all reporting, numbers, charts and sources unchanged. Explain your proposed changes before editing the HTML and CSS. Avoid unnecessary decorative UI.”
  1. Open your published project. Save a before screenshot.
  2. Identify three specific design problems.
  3. Ask AI to implement the improvements you choose.
  4. Keep data, reporting, charts and sources intact.
  5. Test at mobile width. Save the after.
